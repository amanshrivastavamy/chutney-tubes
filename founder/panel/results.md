## What would flip a no

- List it on Blinkit or Zepto so I can add a tube directly to my quick-commerce cart.
- Clear proof that it is preservative-free without synthetic vinegar taste, or a free trial sample.
- A price point around ₹50 to ₹60 and proof of thick consistency without watery separation.
- A 250g or 300g family squeeze pack priced under ₹120.
- Pricing a single tube around ₹59 or offering a multi-tube travel bundle at a discount.
- Nothing really, unless someone blind-tastes me on it and it genuinely tastes like freshly ground mint without vinegar.
- Proof or video reviews demonstrating that the emulsion never separates or squirts watery liquid even after weeks in the fridge.
- Only if I'm travelling alone or staying somewhere without home-cooked food.
- If I ever lived alone away from home without kitchen access, and the price dropped to around ₹40.
- A 250g value pack priced under ₹110, or bringing the 100g tube down to ₹49.
- Free sample demonstrating thick, non-separating consistency with genuine street-style chaat taste.
- Selling a family size on quick commerce apps at a unit rate comparable to standard sauces, under ₹60 for 100g.
