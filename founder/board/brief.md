# Board Brief: Chutney Tubes

## Business Overview
Chutney Tubes is an Indian consumer packaged goods (FMCG) startup concept offering traditional Indian chutneys and condiments in modern, single-hand 100g squeeze tubes with flip-top caps ("no spoon, no spill, no sticky jar").

## The Core Problem Being Solved
1. **Mess and Inconvenience**: In Indian households and lunch boxes, traditional chutneys are sold in heavy glass jars or fragile plastic tubs requiring clean spoons, or flimsy tear-away sachets that spill everywhere.
2. **Portability**: Glass jars cannot travel in backpacks, tiffin bags, or car dashboards without risk of breakage or oil leakage.
3. **Usage Occasions**: Indian consumers consume condiments across 6+ distinct daily eating occasions:
   - Paratha at breakfast
   - Samosa and pakora with 4 PM chai
   - Momos and noodles at night
   - Rolls and frankies on the go
   - Tiffin at school or college/work
   - Sandwiches on road trips and travel

## Product Portfolio & Unit Economics Target
- **Format**: 100g laminated multi-layer barrier squeeze tube with flip-top cap.
- **Formulation**: 100% vegetarian & eggless (green veg dot).
- **Single Tubes (All locked at ₹99)**:
  1. Masala Tomato Ketchup (Mild, savory tomato with roasted cumin & chaat masala)
  2. Tandoori Mayo (Medium, smoky tandoori spiced eggless emulsion)
  3. Mint Mayo (Mild, cool garden pudina blended with creamy mayo)
  4. Garlic Mayo (Mild, mellow roasted garlic cream)
  5. Schezwan Sauce (Hot, fiery red chilli, ginger & Szechuan pepper)
  6. Red Chilli-Garlic Sauce (Hot, sharp garlic and crushed red chilli)
  7. Hari Chutney (Medium, fresh coriander, green chillies & lemon juice)
  8. Khatti-Meethi Imli Chutney (Mild, tangy tamarind, jaggery & dry ginger)
- **Coming Soon Pipeline**: Teekhi Lehsuni Chutney, Momo Chutney, Sweet Mango Chutney.
- **Curated Value Packs**:
  - Chaat Pair (₹179): Hari Chutney + Khatti-Meethi Imli Chutney
  - Desi-Chinese Pair (₹179): Schezwan Sauce + Red Chilli-Garlic Sauce
  - Mayo Trio (₹269): Tandoori Mayo + Mint Mayo + Garlic Mayo
  - Try-All-8 Sampler (₹699): Complete 8-tube collection
- **Shipping Policy**: Free delivery pan-India.

## Current Validation State
The founder has built and deployed a Next.js digital storefront running a fake-door purchase intent test:
- Visitors configure tubes and packs into a real cart.
- Visitors proceed to checkout, enter contact and postal pin code details.
- When pressing "Pay / Complete Order", an honest reveal screen explains that the brand is in pre-launch preparation, confirms no card/UPI charges were deducted, and collects qualitative survey feedback on format enthusiasm and requested future flavours.
- Real-time conversion tracking measures actual drop-offs from landing page to cart add, checkout start, and pay click.
