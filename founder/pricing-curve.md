# Price Sensitivity Analysis (Van Westendorp)

100 complete answers from simulated buyers. Use this to pick what to test, not as the final word.

| Point | Price | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **PMC** (Point of Marginal Cheapness) | **₹45** | Below this, too many think it is too cheap to be good quality |
| **OPP** (Optimal Price Point) | **₹50** | Point of least resistance (equal numbers say too cheap vs too expensive) |
| **IPP** (Indifference Price Point) | **₹70** | As many call it a bargain as call it expensive |
| **PME** (Point of Marginal Expensiveness) | **₹100** | Above this, too many say it is too expensive to buy |
