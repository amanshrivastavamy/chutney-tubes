# Pricing Strategy: Van Westendorp Analysis, Price Ladder & Unit Economics

Price is the fastest lever on profitability and the easiest to misjudge. Grounded in the 100-buyer consumer panel, competitive benchmarks across India, and real FMCG co-packing unit economics, this strategy sets the exact price structure for Chutney Tubes.

---

## 1. Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Findings

From all 100 panel responses ([`founder/pricing-curve.md`](file:///C:/Users/Lenovo/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/chutney-tubes/founder/pricing-curve.md)):

| Metric | Threshold | Strategic Implication |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Point of Marginal Cheapness (PMC)** | **₹45** | Below ₹45, consumers become suspicious of quality, assuming synthetic chemicals, cheap starch fillers, and artificial food coloring. |
| **Optimal Price Point (OPP)** | **₹50** | The price of least resistance where resistance from both sides (too cheap vs. too expensive) is minimized. |
| **Indifference Price Point (IPP)** | **₹70** | Where an equal number of buyers perceive the product as a "bargain" versus "getting expensive." *Crucially, Mother’s Recipe 200g pouch is anchored at ₹70.* |
| **Point of Marginal Expensiveness (PME)** | **₹100** | The psychological ceiling; above ₹100, mass market resistance spikes violently. |

### The Verdict on the Original ₹99 Price:
At ₹99 for a single 100g tube, the product sits at **₹99 / ₹100 (99% of the PME ceiling)**. 
While 13% of affluent/early-adopter buyers were willing to trial it, **31 out of 100 panelists rejected the product purely on price per gram**, pointing out that ₹99 for 100g equals **₹990 per kilogram**—nearly 3× the cost of Mother’s Recipe (₹350/kg) and Ching's Secret (₹340/kg).

## 2. Competitive Price & Unit Cost Reality

### A. Factory Gate Variable Costs (Per 100g Tube)
* **Raw Ingredients (Fresh Coriander, Mint, Tamarind, Jaggery, Spices)**: ₹12.00 – ₹16.00
* **Multi-Layer Barrier Laminate Tube + Flip-top Cap + Induction Foil**: ₹7.50 – ₹9.50
* **Contract Filling, Sealing & QA Batch Testing**: ₹4.00 – ₹5.50
* **Inner Cardboard Box & Protective Sleeve**: ₹3.50 – ₹4.50
* **Total COGS (Factory Gate)**: **~₹31.00 per tube**

### B. The Channel Mathematics
* **The Fatal Single-Tube D2C Sale at ₹99**:
  * Gross Revenue: ₹99.00
  * Payment Gateway Fee (2%): -₹2.00
  * Pan-India Courier Shipping: -₹55.00
  * Warehouse Pick, Pack & Outer Box: -₹15.00
  * COGS: -₹31.00
  * **Net Contribution Before CAC: -₹4.00 (Loss of ₹4 per order before spending a single rupee on ads!)**
  * With digital CAC of ₹150–₹250, every single-tube sale destroys ₹154 to ₹254 of founder capital.

* **The Quick Commerce Dark Store Channel (Zepto / Blinkit) at ₹79 MRP**:
  * Retail MRP: ₹79.00
  * Quick Commerce Platform Margin (30%–32%): -₹25.00
  * Inwarding & Regional Logistics: -₹4.00
  * COGS: -₹31.00
  * **Net Contribution Margin per Tube: +₹19.00 (24% net margin)**
  * Quick Commerce absorbs last-mile 10-minute delivery; customers buy on impulse without 3-day shipping friction.

## 3. Recommended Price Strategy

### 1. Re-anchor the Single Tube at ₹79 MRP (Quick Commerce Exclusive)
* Move the single 100g tube price from ₹99 down to **₹79**. 
* ₹79 sits comfortably inside the acceptable range (between IPP ₹70 and PME ₹100), eliminating the "₹100 sticker shock" while preserving a healthy 24% net distributor margin.
* **Rule**: Never sell a standalone single tube on the D2C website with free shipping.

Marketing and landing page copy must proactively dismantle the panel's top 3 verbatim price objections:

1. *"100g finishes in 4 breakfasts, costing ₹25 per meal just for chutney."*

3. *"Why order online and wait 3 days for chutney?"*  
   ➔ **Marketing Counter**: Highlight the pantry habit: *"Keep the Chaat Pair in your office desk drawer or backpack. Ready whenever 4 PM samosas arrive."*
